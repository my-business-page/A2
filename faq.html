Where is Soft Hour?

In Yew Tee, Singapore. The current pickup listing is 680543, level 12, first unit on the left, next to Yew Tee CC.

Is it dine-in?

No. Soft Hour is a takeaway-only home cafe.

When are you open?

Hours can change. Check @hello.softhour on Instagram before visiting.

How do I order?

Use the live ordering page on Tabaous. Ordering is handled online and pickup follows the instructions shown at checkout.

Do you serve coffee?

The current menu is focused on matcha, hojicha and tea-based drinks.

Can I customise sweetness?

Customisation options should be checked on the current ordering menu because availability can change.

Are seasonal drinks always available?

No. Seasonal and limited drinks may sell out or rotate.
