03 / community

Made in Yew Tee.
For Yew Tee.

Soft Hour is a home cafe run by Andy and Shi Lin. Their goal is not only to earn from the neighbourhood, but to create a community within it.

soft
hour

the people behind the window

Andy + Shi Lin

They built Soft Hour around the freedom to experiment, make drinks they love and remember the people who return. The little window is intentionally personal.

One of their community ideas is a run club, where people can come together and share a cup of matcha afterwards.

01

Remember names.

Regulars become familiar faces. The owners make an effort to remember customers and their stories.

02

Make room.

Small gestures, notes and conversations are part of what Soft Hour hopes to grow locally.

03

Keep creating.

New recipes and playful flavours give the owners space to explore their creativity.
