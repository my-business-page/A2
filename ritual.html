02 / our ritual

A cup is a
little sequence.

Not rushed. Not overworked. Just a few deliberate movements that turn powder, water and milk into something worth pausing for.

01

Sift

Start with the matcha itself. Fine, even and ready to meet water.

02

Water

A measured pour. Enough to wake the tea without drowning its character.

03

Whisk

The bamboo whisk moves quickly until the surface turns smooth and alive.

04

Pour

Ice, milk, fruit or foam — the final layer depends on the drink you chose.
